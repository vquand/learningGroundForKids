# Town Lake

Status: implemented locally; awaiting user UAT. Updated 2026-10-24.
Branch: `feature/town-lake`, based on local `main` at `3e986e2`.

## Objective and confirmed behavior

Extend each child's Town with a permanently unlocked lake map after the server
verifies Flower prosperity (stage 3). Lower prosperity after relocating or
removing buildings must never revoke the unlock. Existing Flower towns qualify
when first loaded by the new implementation; do not fabricate historical unlock
dates for towns that no longer qualify and have no recorded unlock.

- Show a big cute arrow at the Town river's right edge after unlocking. It jumps
  once every five seconds while visible, with a still reduced-motion version.
  Clicking or keyboard activation opens the lake. Provide a visible return to
  Town at the incoming river and preserve each map's viewing position.
- The lake has approximately 15% land on its left and 85% water. The Town river
  enters from the left; an outgoing river leaves on the right. No further map or
  onward travel is available. Preserve Town's existing terrain and placements.
- Any existing Town building that fits may move to the lake shore. Share the
  child's Town storage and preserve building identity, inhabitants, paid recipe,
  and earned achievements through relocation.
- Three raft categories: contained fish, freely roaming fish with a home, and
  aquatic plants supported by a floating structure. Rafts can be built before
  any of their fish options unlock, using valid empty appearances. The friendly
  raft has its own permanent blueprint unlock at 100 individual nursery fish
  harvested; it must be unlocked and built before it can receive fish.
- Underwater fish use animated shadow sprite sheets. Occasionally fish surface,
  feed, jump, or play, with species-appropriate variations. Fish belonging to an
  open home swim around this lake and return; they do not enter either river.
- Species unlock separately from buildings using verified Fishing Friends
  catches, with ten carp catches as the user's example. Reuse existing fish
  names/identifiers where suitable. Existing catches should count.
- Use wood and small, meaningful metal/wool amounts for construction. Metal can
  represent nails, brackets and anchors. Wool is for decorative pennants or
  cushions, never represented as durable submerged netting or mooring rope.
- Growth is included in the first version and advances only during active lake
  viewing. Results include Town stamina, additional friendly-raft fish, and
  collection progress. Choosing residents after their species unlock is free;
  never debit the Fishing Friends bucket to stock a raft.
- Mature fish leave nursery slots when harvested. If no compatible friendly
  raft is built or has space, keep them in a separate lake collection reserve.
  Weather and resting hours affect behavior only; they never slow or pause
  growth while the lake is actively viewed.
- Lake development uses fish egg → small fish → cute chubby fish, analogous to
  Town's seed/sprout/bud/flower indicator. This describes overall map development,
  separately from each resident's individual growth.
- Each fish raft holds up to 16 small fish or four big fish, according to the
  fish type. Capacity category belongs to the species, not its current growth
  frame: a young big-species fish still uses a big-fish slot.
- Different fish/plants have shallow/deep habitat requirements. The middle of
  the lake may occupy 50% of the total map surface as deep water; the remaining
  surface is approximately 15% land and 35% shallow water. Raft designs can
  independently require shore access. Validate all requirements on the server.
- Fish have different morning, afternoon, evening or nocturnal activity, using
  the server's saved home-city clock. Lake season, rain, snow, snow cover,
  day/night lighting and weather freshness match the first map. Live weather
  changes fish behavior as well as appearance.
- Provide insect raisers/attractors as standalone one-by-one shore or floating
  buildings. Selecting either shows a feeding
  radius circle. Fish within any qualifying circle grow 20% faster: maximum
  multiplier 1.2, never 1.4 or 1.44 in overlapping circles.
- Include a named lake achievement group, matching badge artwork, explicit
  conditions and difficulty, and complete Parent/Admin child reporting.

## Research and proposed roster

The fishing catalog uses playful common names rather than exact scientific
species. These are game adaptations of the real structures and behavior below;
do not silently assign precise biological species to generic catalog fish.

| Structure | Initial fish/plant proposal | Real structure and visual adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish nursery raft | Pebble Carp `P3`; Whisker Catfish `R4` | Rounded wood/bamboo floating frame, visible submerged net bag, small mooring floats and metal brackets. Species selection changes underwater silhouette and nursery detail. |
| Friendly raft | Pebble Carp `P3`; Ribbon Koi `P5`; Round Bluegill `P4`; Button Minnow `P1` | Open feeding platform with no enclosing net. Carp/koi use shade and a feeding ring; bluegill/minnows use visible hanging woody shelter and plant roots. Keep a common raft category but adapt its fitted habitat to the selected fish. |
| Floating garden | Water spinach; soft rush, with blue flag iris as a possible flowering alternative | Buoyant wood/bamboo frame, planting pockets/baskets and visible hanging roots. Leafy crop and wetland flower appearances must have appropriate root supports. |

Proposed fish unlock: ten lifetime verified catches of that exact catalog ID,
permanently unlocked, independent of current inventory and raft construction.
Display progress such as `Pebble Carp · 6/10 caught`; allow inspecting locked
species. No fish generated in the lake counts toward Fishing Friends catches,
points, bait, location graduation or these ten-catch requirements.
Free stocking does not add to lifetime catches or Fishing Friends inventory.

Research basis:

- [FAO cage design](https://www.fao.org/4/ad021e/ad021e01.htm) describes buoyant
  collars with suspended synthetic-fiber netting. [FAO species review](https://www.fao.org/4/t8598e/t8598e05.htm)
  includes carp and catfish in cage systems. These support the nursery concept;
  wool is not a substitute for actual aquaculture netting.
- [FWC freshwater fish attractors](https://myfwc.com/fishing/freshwater/sites-forecasts/fish-attractors/)
  documents woody shelter, minnows and bluegill around attractors. An open refuge
  is a better fit than a cage with an imaginary invisible wall.
- [University of Minnesota carp research](https://maisrc.umn.edu/carp-biocontrol)
  documents attraction to learned feeding sites, including field trials. This
  supports carp returning for food. Scheduled returns of every individual,
  friendly jumping and play remain storybook simplifications. Koi behavior is
  an ornamental-carp game adaptation, not a claim that a documented fish species
  instinctively lives on a raft.
- [NC State floating wetlands guide](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/installation-and-maintenance-of-floating-treatment-wetlands)
  describes buoyant frames, planting pockets and suspended roots, and lists soft
  rush and blue flag iris. [JIRCAS water-spinach profile](https://www.jircas.go.jp/en/database/thaivege/059)
  describes floating stems, wet habitats and harvested shoots. Keep cultivated
  plants inside their supported raft footprint.

Leave trout, perch, dace, pike and goldfish out of this initial roster to keep
the habitat/art work bounded. This is a scope proposal, not a finding that they
cannot be kept or attracted in real life. Turtles retain their fishing release
behavior and are not stocked as fish.

## Construction balance

The user confirmed that the new lake building types average about 20% more
construction materials than original Town buildings. Compare wood, rock, sand,
metal and wool totals, excluding stamina, trees, roads and fences. The original
14 buildings plus vegetable garden total 385 units: 25.67 on average. The five
lake blueprints total 155 units: 31 on average, or **20.8% higher**. Individual
lake recipes are 30–32 units. Existing Town blueprints keep their own recipes
when built on the lake shore; relocation uses the recorded original paid cost.
Rafts occupy three columns by two rows. The shore insect raiser is one tile.

| Raft | Stamina | Wood | Rock | Metal | Wool |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fish nursery | 8 | 23 | 4 | 4 | 0 |
| Friendly raft | 6 | 22 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| Floating garden | 5 | 23 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Shore insect raiser | 3 | 24 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Floating insect raiser | 4 | 25 | 3 | 3 | 1 |

Rock represents mooring ballast or foundations; metal is brackets/nails and
wool is above-water binding/decorative fabric. Fish rafts have no new feeder
attachment action; feeding comes from standalone insect buildings.
No daily decay, repairs, death, sickness, feed inventory or offline penalties are
proposed. Friendly-raft construction is independently locked until 100 nursery
fish have been harvested. Use existing move pricing (half of original paid materials, each
rounded up) for relocations, no-op/cancellation free, no demolition refund.
Cross-map relocation must be one atomic action, not remove-then-build.

## Depth, habitat and shore access

Represent land, protected river mouths, shallow water and deep water with shared
browser-safe authoritative geometry. Deep water occupies the middle 50% of total
map area, not 50% of the remaining water. Use a calm, distinguishable deeper
water color without realistic textures or a distracting hard grid in viewing.
Its two depth borders have deterministic zigzags of varied spacing and depth,
shared by rendering and habitat checks. Deep water remains one connected body
around the island, approximately half of the active grid (currently 391 tiles).

Each raft blueprint specifies allowed depth(s) and whether it needs shore
access; species selection supplies compatible habitat/depth requirements too.
Validate build, move, populate and replant changes with the combined
rules. An empty raft being buildable never makes every species valid there.
For shore-required structures, require a cardinally adjacent unobstructed land
access tile. Diagonal contact is insufficient. Show invalid-footprint previews
and specific localized reasons before spending. Mid-lake installations use the
chosen design's anchoring/access arrangements rather than an invented universal
shore-adjacency rule. Keep incoming/outgoing river passages clear.

Real-informed initial examples, still roster proposals:

- Nursery cages need deep-water clearance for their suspended net bags, even
  when their fish also naturally forage in shallow water. [FAO carp cage
  guidance](https://www.fao.org/fishery/docs/DOCUMENT/sec/CAFCAC_2011/4e.pdf)
  explicitly calls for water several meters deep. Do not label all carp as
  obligately deep-water fish merely because their cage needs clearance.
- Bluegill/minnow open shelters favor shallow, covered water. Carp/koi use a
  compatible feeding/shade habitat. Catfish nursery design uses deep water and
  a darker refuge. These are simplified gameplay habitats, not claims that
  real fish never cross depth boundaries.
- Floating water-spinach/rush supports can work above shallow or deep water
  when their roots hang from a raft. A possible water-lily option instead
  requires shallow rooted support: [Minnesota DNR](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/aquatic_plants/floatingleaf_plants/white_water_lily.html)
  describes white water lilies rooted in bottoms up to five feet deep. Do not
  invent a deep-water requirement for a surface-floating plant.

## Building relocation and map-aware simulation

No blueprint whitelist: any unlocked existing building/tree/garden/road/fence
may be built or moved onto valid land if its complete footprint fits. Existing
construction, movement, learning, resource and collision rules still apply.
Buildings cannot cover water, block protected passages or extend off-map.

Every entity belongs to one map; legacy entities default to Town. Transfer is
revision-checked and atomic, preserving identity, residents, pets, crops,
original paid costs and lifetime achievements. A failed move changes neither
map nor balance. Do not duplicate a building to show it on both maps.

Generalize business road/arrival paths, walking, pets, animal contacts and garden
simulation to the entity's map terrain and land entry. Existing ready businesses
remain ready after relocation; new arrivals require valid map-local access.
Preserve business interiors, help and opening hours on both maps. Never combine
two maps' coordinate-equal roads, fences, animals or garden patches into one
collision/pathfinding world. Lake fish/plant growth requires active lake viewing;
navigation must checkpoint and pause the departing map without offline catch-up.

## Fish behavior, weather and insect feeding

Use a per-species habitat record with size class, suitable structures/depths,
shelter preference, activity windows, food profile and weather responses. Generic
catalog names are game characters inspired by documented fish, not precision
species identifications. Assigning one narrow daily period is a gameplay
simplification; many real fish feed across multiple periods.

Proposed starting activity: carp/bluegill morning and late afternoon/evening,
koi/minnows daylight with calmer midday behavior, catfish strongest in evening
and night with daytime refuge resting. [Texas A&M catfish guide](https://fisheries.tamu.edu/pond-management/species/channel-catfish/)
describes deeper daytime shelter and dusk/night activity. [Minnesota DNR
bluegill profile](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/minnaqua/speciesprofile/bluegill.html)
notes feeding throughout the day with morning/evening fishing peaks. Exact game
windows must be authored explicitly; do not claim afternoon-only behavior
without species evidence. Use existing homeClock periods: morning 05–11, noon
11–14, afternoon 14–17, evening 17–20, night 20–05. Missing live clock must not
invent an eligible feeding period.

Calm/light-rain water may permit gentle surface feeding; heavier rain favors
shelter/deeper cruising and fewer jumps. Winter/snow favors calmer sheltered
motion. Do not freeze over moving rivers or remove fish because a cosmetic snow
layer appears. Use only supported live weather fields; do not fabricate storm
intensity if the existing provider projection only distinguishes rain/clear.
Weather-aware behavior and any growth eligibility must use live server data,
never cosmetic admin previews. Loss of weather data must not be treated as a
known sunny day or erase progress. Reuse weather freshness/fallback rules from
Town. Sleep, weather shelter and fish paths must not reset on each heartbeat.

Bluegill is the strongest insect-food match in the proposed roster. [Texas A&M
bluegill guide](https://fisheries.tamu.edu/pond-management/species/bluegill/)
identifies insects and invertebrates as most of its diet. [FAO carp profile](https://www.fao.org/fishery/docs/CDrom/aquaculture/I1129m/file/en/en_commoncarp.htm)
describes an omnivore eating insects, larvae and other foods. Catfish also eat
insects within a broader diet. Minnow needs depend on the actual species; do
not claim all minnows are obligate insect feeders. Black-soldier-fly larvae are
a plausible raised supplemental feed: [FAO insect-feed overview](https://www.fao.org/edible-insects/home/insects-for-feed/en).
The researched real feed supports the design; it does not imply every species
requires an insect building or an insect-only diet.

Use one shared insect-feeding system in two forms:

- Shore building: exactly one-by-one land tile, cardinally adjacent to usable
  water, with the same terrain/cost/revision validation as construction.
- Floating building: one-by-one water tile, unlocked after 16 nursery fish
  harvested. It shares the shore building's feeding behavior. No new module
  can be added directly to a fish raft; older saved modules remain compatible.
- Clicking a source shows its circular feeding range; deselection hides it.
  Circle center/radius come from the same server-owned geometry as coverage,
  transform correctly with map camera/zoom, and do not cover action buttons.
- During active verified simulation, an eligible fish inside one or more
  functioning sources receives exactly 1.2 times its normal growth rate. Two
  raisers, two attractors, or one of each still give only +20%. The bonus changes
  growth speed, not payout size, catch odds, fish capacity or plant growth.
- Compute coverage from each fixed raft center, never browser coordinates or
  a claimed nearby boolean. No retroactive boost when a source is built or
  moved. Fish positions and decorative motion run entirely in the browser.
- Keep insect motion and feeding cute and brief; no graphic prey animation.
  Do not require night visits, introduce starvation/death, or farm feed while
  the map is closed. Optional feed storage/depletion and exact radius remain
  design choices below; a plain source aura can avoid a new inventory economy.

## Individual growth and lake development

Simple active growth/rewards are approved. Free stocking after the verified
species unlock is approved, subject to the selected raft's blueprint being
unlocked and built. Nursery capacity is 16 small fish or four big fish;
use the same fish-type capacity for friendly rafts. Proposed size classes:
Button Minnow and Round Bluegill are small; Pebble Carp, Ribbon Koi and Whisker
Catfish are big. This is a game capacity classification, not mature body-size
equivalence. Proposed initial model is one species per raft, avoiding ambiguous
mixed-size capacity. Growth does not change its capacity class.

Use a manual Collect action after each short, server-timed growth cycle.
Harvesting removes mature residents from nursery slots, grants the verified
stamina result once, and increments lifetime lake harvest progress by the number
of fish, not the number of button presses. Fish leave the nursery rather than
remaining as breeders that generate additional copies.

The friendly raft permanently unlocks at 100 total individual fish harvested
from nursery rafts. Until it is built with compatible free slots, harvested
fish stay in the lake collection reserve. This reserve is independent of the
Fishing Friends bucket; no catch, bait, graduation or fishing reward is generated.
The 100th harvest succeeds without an existing friendly raft. Show the unlock
and leave construction to the normal material-checked build action.

After construction, let the child place compatible reserved mature fish into
the friendly raft or choose free newly stocked residents under the earlier
free-stocking decision. Mature reserve transfers preserve the fish's growth;
free residents begin their own growth. Neither path can overfill capacity.
Moving fish between reserve and friendly homes, or collecting friendly fish,
must not count as a new nursery harvest or grant repeated stamina. Never erase
an existing resident to make room. The nursery may be freely restocked with the
already-unlocked species after its slots are vacated.

For example, a single newly committed harvest of four big nursery fish changes
96 harvested to 100, removes those four nursery residents, adds four to the
reserve, and unlocks the friendly blueprint. Retrying that harvest changes
nothing. Building a compatible friendly raft and transferring those four fish
leaves the lifetime count at 100 and grants no second harvest reward.

Plants regrow after collection. Growth proceeds at base rate during resting
hours and all weather while this map is actively viewed; feeding coverage is
the only proposed +20% multiplier. Exact duration/payout and compatible transfer
roster remain balance proposals, not gaps in the agreed lifecycle.

Map development proposal:

| Stage | Visual | Proposed verified milestone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Fish egg | Lake unlocked; beginning its development |
| 1 | Small fish | First occupied fish raft or planted garden |
| 2 | Cute chubby fish | All three raft categories developed, three fish types settled, and ten growth collections |

These milestones represent map development, not the age or size class of every
fish. Recommend permanent highest-earned lake stages so reorganizing rafts
does not erase visible development. The original Town prosperity rules remain
independent; moving businesses must not revoke the durable lake unlock.

## Project structure and technology

Reuse React 19, Vite 8, TypeScript, the Town canvas/art infrastructure, Hono,
PostgreSQL and existing authenticated same-origin clients. No new dependencies
are proposed. Relevant code:

- `frontend/src/features/town-building/`: common Town shell, navigation,
  construction controls, storage and reward bar; common rules in `AGENTS.md`.
- `frontend/src/features/town-building/maps/`: common map integration rules in
  `AGENTS.md`; each map has an implementation directory and its own linked guide.
- `frontend/src/features/town-building/maps/lake/`: separate lake scene, panels,
  styling, fish/plant presentation and lake-specific artwork adapters. Its
  `AGENTS.md` links both common Town and shared map instructions.
- `server/src/town-building/`: common storage, transactions, construction,
  authentication integration and live environment; common rules in `AGENTS.md`.
- `server/src/town-building/maps/`: common map contracts and inheritance rules.
- `server/src/town-building/maps/lake/`: lake terrain/depth, unique unlocks,
  habitat catalogs, growth/feeding simulation and lake achievement definitions,
  with its own `AGENTS.md` referencing the common rules.
- `server/src/fishing/`: authoritative catch/inventory integration; retain its
  revisioned command and catch reward behavior.
- `server/src/achievement-catalog.ts` and `server/src/parent-reports/`: catalog
  migration and complete child reports in both spaces.
- `frontend/public/minigames/town-building/`: authored sprite atlases and asset
  metadata following the existing private artwork pipeline.
- Existing `frontend/tests/`, `server/tests/` and guarded database tests for
  source tests; all generated verification evidence stays under `/tmp`.

Prefer extending existing Town JSON for unlock/map membership and lake state;
do not assume a SQL migration is needed. Inspect concurrency and legacy-state
normalization before fixing the storage design. Existing entities without map
membership remain in Town. Map-specific collision and terrain checks must never
change the protected original Town river mask.

### Map boundaries for future maps

Use stable allowlisted map IDs and an explicit map definition/context for
terrain, dimensions, protected areas, entry points, placements and scenery.
An entity has one map and one footprint. Shared services receive this context
rather than branching on map names or hard-coding Town terrain into every path.
Every future `maps/<map-id>/` directory must add its own `AGENTS.md` linking the
common Town and map guides before brainstorming or implementation.

Keep common reusable code in the Town feature, extracting only what the lake
slice actually shares. Preserve existing original-map files/routes while
introducing the boundary; do not relocate the entire existing feature merely
to make the directory tree symmetrical. New maps get isolated runtime modules
and scene adapters, not copies of authentication, economy or reward services.

## Code style and authority

Follow existing typed, pure domain helpers and revisioned commands. For example,
the existing client sends intent, not balances or eligibility:

```ts
export const actTown = (id: string, revision: number, command: TownCommand) =>
    coreRequest<TownResponse>(`${base(id)}/action`, {
        method: 'POST', body: JSON.stringify({ revision, command }),
    });
```

Use server-owned fish/habitat allowlists and derive lifetime catches from durable
catch provenance, not the browser or current spendable fish inventory. Stocking
is free after unlocking: never debit or mutate Fishing Friends quantities just
to populate a lake raft. Preserve fishing stats/discoveries and avoid inverse
locking order relative to existing fishing-to-Town reward transactions.
Reload/retry/multi-tab activity must not duplicate fish, buildings or stamina.

## Boundaries and acceptance criteria

Always authenticate ownership and verify costs, terrain, capacity and eligibility
on the server. Keep same-origin traffic and the private artwork pipeline. Keep
UI/animation cosmetic, with server receipts driving any stamina reward flights.
Always preserve existing saved Town/Fishing state and source-game reward rates.

Raft fish capacities, free stocking, unrestricted fitting-building transfers,
separate map modules/guides, removal of harvested nursery residents, the
100-nursery-fish friendly-raft unlock, and behavior-only weather/resting effects
are user decisions; do not revert them to earlier alternatives.
Never provision cloud resources, modify production data, commit, push, merge or
publish before the user's required `confirmed` instruction.

Acceptance includes permanent Flower unlock; incoming/outgoing rivers and left
shore composition; bounded accessible navigation; three distinct real-inspired
raft categories; verified species gating with existing catches; correct atomic
shared storage and relocation; species shadow/surface art; universal badges and
both reports; all applicable checks and viewport verification passing.

## Remaining balance proposals

- Five active minutes for an unboosted growth cycle and a four-tile insect
  feeding radius. The +20% multiplier, single stacking limit and 16/4 capacities
  are fixed user decisions. Stamina reward values remain whole-number proposals.
- One species per raft; species-appropriate compatible transfer destinations.
- Permanent highest-earned lake development stages with the milestones above.

No core clarification questions remain. Review the concrete balance/roster and
implementation plan alongside this spec; no release approval is implied.


### Frontend-owned movement and project board

Swimming, surface jumps, rest poses and movement loops run from the browser
animation clock; the server receives no per-frame positions. Fish feeding uses
the fixed raft center, so server growth needs no movement simulation. The server
returns the wooden development-board goals only when a child opens a Town or
Lake stage icon. The board lists concrete tasks, item counts and materials,
never the internal prosperity scoring formula.
