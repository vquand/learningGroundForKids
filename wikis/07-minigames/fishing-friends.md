# Fishing Friends

**Fishing Friends** (`/fishing`) is a relaxing ecology and timing game where children explore freshwater ecosystems, master casting mechanics, and collect diverse native fish species for their Town Lake.

---

## 🎣 Locations & Ecosystems

1. **The Meadow Pond**:
   - Calm, shallow water suitable for beginners.
   - Home to Carp, Goldfish, Tilapia, and Pond Frogs.
2. **The Fast River**:
   - Swifter current requiring precise reel timing and specialized bait.
   - Home to River Trout, Catfish, Salmon, and River Eels.
3. **The Town Lake (Unlocked via Fishing Achievements)**:
   - Catching fish across the pond and river unlocks fish species in the player's personal Town Lake.

---

## ⚡ Energy & Bait Mechanics

- **Stamina System**:
  - Casting line expends stamina.
  - Stamina regenerates over time or recharges instantly by eating farm meals or collecting goods from the river trade raft (`town_raft_items`).
- **Bait Crafting**:
  - Breadcrumbs, Worms, Corn, and Shiny Spinners appeal to distinct fish species.
- **Timing Reel Mini-Event**:
  - When a fish bites, a tension gauge appears. Keeping the bobber in the green sweet spot lands the catch.

---

## 🏅 Fishing Achievements

From the `fishing` achievement group:
- **First Catch**: Land 1st fish.
- **Pond Angler**: Reach 500 pond points.
- **River Master**: Catch 10 distinct river species.
- **Lake Friend**: Catch every native freshwater species and populate the Town Lake.
