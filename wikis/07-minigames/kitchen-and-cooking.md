# Kitchen & Cooking Simulations

The **Kitchen & Cooking** suite (`/kitchen`, `/cooking`, `/cooking/cabbage-rolls`, `/cooking/hamburger`) introduces culinary chemistry, step-by-step procedure following, and measurement precision through interactive cooking stations.

---

## 🍲 Featured Culinary Modules

### 1. Cabbage Roll Kitchen (`cabbage-rolls`)
A complete 6-step Vietnamese/traditional culinary simulation:
1. **Leaf Selection & Blanching**: Softening cabbage leaves in hot water.
2. **Filling Preparation**: Seasoning minced pork, wood-ear mushrooms, and pepper.
3. **Rolling & Wrapping**: Folding and tying neat parcels.
4. **Broth Seasoning**: Measuring tomato, shallot, and fish sauce broth.
5. **Simmering & Heat Control**: Managing the flame timer (undercooked vs perfect vs burnt).
6. **Plating & Serving**: Garnishing with scallions and serving three rolls.

*Server-Verified Outcome*:
- `Roll Chef`: Complete all six steps.
- `A Little Too Soon`: Undercooked simmering result.
- `Toasty Lesson`: Burnt simmering result.
- `Perfectly Simmered`: Perfect golden simmering timing.

### 2. Burger Kitchen (`burger`)
Assembly and order fulfillment simulation:
- Reading incoming orders (e.g., "Brioche bun, cheddar, beef patty, pickles, lettuce, Dijon mustard").
- Stacking ingredients in exact sequential order under time constraints.

### 3. Food & Drink Bar (`food-and-drink`)
Crafting traditional teas, fruit juices, and smoothies with fruit chopping and blending stages.
